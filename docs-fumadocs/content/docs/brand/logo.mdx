The @lifeatjntuh logo: the pink `@` ring mark plus the "lifeatjntuh"
wordmark in Fredoka with a pink `@`. Light tone (plum text) for
cream/pastel backgrounds; dark tone (white text) for plum surfaces and
photos.

## The @lifeatjntuh pill

Sign every post with the **@lifeatjntuh pill** — a fully round pill
with the handle in it. It is the signature on every post, carousel end
slide and story.

The construction: the logo mark in a white circle (56u, 3u padding),
the handle in Fredoka semibold (34u) with a hot-pink `@`, 14u gap
between mark and text. Light tone on pastel and cream — nearly opaque
white, `bg-white/85` in the website repo (`rgba(255,255,255,0.88)` in
the studio canvas). Dark tone on photos and plum — `bg-white/15` +
`backdrop-blur` (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, blur 6px in the studio).
The full treatment, with the glass values, is on the
[Social primitives](/docs/social-media-kit/primitives) page — the pill
is the house glass reference.

## Rules

- Don't stretch or squash the logo or photos.
- Don't recolour the `@` — it stays hot pink.
- The website keeps its mark as a local media asset, so the package
  does not bundle one — pass it as `markSrc` to the
  [Logo component](/docs/components/logo) when you have
  it; without it, only the wordmark renders.
