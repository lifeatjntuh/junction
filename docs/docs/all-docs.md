# All docs

Every article in the Junction docs, grouped the way the sidebar groups them. On mobile the sidebar is hidden, so this page — and the same list in the footer of every page — is the bird-eye view.

## Brand

- [Junction](intro.md) — the brand design guidelines for LifeatJNTUH, with the design system, the Social Media Kit and the App Design Kit as parts of it.
- [Brand](brand/index.md) — what Junction is: one look for the site, socials and apps.
- [Colours](brand/colours.md) — Plum, Cream, Hot pink, Orange, Violet, and six pastels.
- [Typography](brand/typography.md) — Fredoka website headlines, Bricolage Grotesque social headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans body, Lexend app screens.
- [Logo](brand/logo.md) — the pink @ ring mark, the Fredoka wordmark, and the @lifeatjntuh pill that signs every post.
- [Shapes](brand/shapes.md) — 32px panel corners, fully round buttons and chips, soft plum-tinted shadows.
- [Photography](brand/photography.md) — real campus photos, always credited; no stock photos of other campuses.
- [Rules](brand/rules.md) — the do's and don'ts every post, story and screen must follow.

## Design System

- [Design System](design-system/index.md) — the @lifeatjntuh/junction npm package: components, primitives, tokens and theme.
