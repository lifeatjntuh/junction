# Rules

The do's and don'ts from the design system. Every post, story and
screen must follow them.

## Do

- One pastel or one solid colour per post.
- Hot pink is the highlight — one loud colour, not the background.
- Real campus photos, always with the photographer's credit.
- One 3D object per post at most, placed away from the text.
- Sign every post with the @lifeatjntuh pill.
- Mention the WhatsApp Channel for people who aren't on Instagram.
- Grotesk for the big social headline, round font for captions.
- Keep the top 250px and bottom 300px of stories clear for app controls.
- Use Solar icons, linear, everywhere.

## Don't

- Don't mix two headline fonts in the same headline.
- Don't name admins — LifeOps credit goes to codenames.
- Don't stretch or squash the logo or photos.
- Don't use stock photos of other campuses.
- Don't put two pastels fighting on one surface.
- Don't use thick outlines — soft shadows instead.
