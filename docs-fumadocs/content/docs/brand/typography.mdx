Two headline fonts with clear jobs, and one body font for everything
else.

## Website headlines — Fredoka

<HeadingDemo />

Fredoka, 600 to 700. Sentence case. Friendly and soft. Use it for the
website and for warm captions on social.

## Scale specimens

The website design-system page shows both scales side by side:

**Round scale (Fredoka), on a pink panel:**

- Section title — large
- Card title — small

**Grotesk scale (Bricolage Grotesque), on a lime panel:**

And the three font specimen cards: "Find your people" in Fredoka 6xl
on white; "Open mic night" in grotesk on dark; a body sample in Plus

The same sizes exist in the package as the
[Heading](/docs/components/heading) sizes (display / xl / lg / md /
sm / xs) — the specimens above are what those sizes look like in the
brand's own words.

## Social headlines — Bricolage Grotesque

Bricolage Grotesque, extra bold, condensed, all caps. Loud and quick
to read on a phone. Keep it under six words — see the grotesk examples
in the Heading demo above.

## Body and buttons — Plus Jakarta Sans

<TextDemo />

Plus Jakarta Sans, 400 for text, 600 to 700 for buttons, chips and
labels.

## App screens — Lexend

Lexend for app screens (Event Tracker, Buddy apps): headings, buttons,
tabs and labels. See the [App Design Kit](/docs/app-design-kit).

## Rules

- Don't mix two headline fonts in the same headline.
- Grotesk headlines: under six words.
- Fredoka headlines: sentence case, not all caps.
