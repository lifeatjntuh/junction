**Junction is the brand design guidelines for LifeatJNTUH** — one look
for the site, our socials and our apps. The design system (the
components in the `@lifeatjntuh/junction` package) is **one part** of
Junction, not the whole of it.

The design system itself — the components in the `@lifeatjntuh/junction` package — was designed by [@muddassirhq](https://github.com/muddassirhq), its sole author.

One look: Plum and cream do most of the work, hot pink is the one loud
colour, pastels fill panels and backgrounds. Fredoka for website
headlines, Bricolage Grotesque for social headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans
for body, Lexend for app screens. 32px panel corners, fully round
buttons and chips, soft shadows, no thick outlines. Solar icons,
linear, everywhere.
