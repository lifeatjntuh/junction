# Junction — the brand guidelines

Junction is the **brand design guidelines for LifeatJNTUH** — one look
for the site, our socials and our apps. The design system (the
components in this package) is **one part** of Junction, not the whole
of it.

The design system itself was designed by [@muddassirhq](https://github.com/muddassirhq), its sole author.

- **Source:** `lifeatjntuh-admin/website` —
  `src/components/pages/design-system/page.tsx` (+ `app-kit.tsx`),
  and the website's `src/components/ui/` and `src/components/app/`
  components.
- **Live reference:** https://lifeatjntuh.web.app/design-system
- **Package:** `@lifeatjntuh/junction` (0.1.0, not published yet —
  see [Installation](../installation.md)).

## The look, in one paragraph

One look for the site and our socials: Plum and cream do most of the
work, hot pink is the one loud colour, pastels fill panels and
backgrounds. Fredoka for website headlines, Bricolage Grotesque for
social headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans for body, Lexend for app screens.
32px panel corners, fully round buttons and chips, soft shadows, no
thick outlines. Solar icons, linear, everywhere.

## Brand story and voice

LifeatJNTUH is the independent student platform for JNTUH life —
started in 2022, run by students, for students. The voice is direct,
practical and plain: simple English, no corporate jargon, no grand
branding. Posts sound like a helpful senior, not an institution.
Content is in English first (~60%), with Telugu (~20%) and Hindi
(~20%) where it fits the audience.

The look follows the voice: friendly and soft, never loud all over.
One pastel or one solid colour per post; hot pink is the highlight,
not the background. Real campus photos carry the story; shiny 3D
objects add the fun — at most one per post.
