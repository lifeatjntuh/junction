Real campus photos carry the story. Use real photos from JNTUH —
never stock photos of other campuses, never screenshots of text.

## Photo of the Day

The [Photo of the Day](/docs/social-media-kit/posts) is the full-bleed
photo post: the photo fills the whole frame edge to edge, nothing on
it but the @lifeatjntuh handle and the photographer's credit. No
title, no caption card, no chips. **Portrait 1080×1350 — never
1080×1080 for a single post** (1080×1350 / 4:5 is the ratio that has
become the norm for feed posts recently). The same full-bleed
treatment applies to carousel photo slides (1080×1350) and story
photos (1080×1920).

## Credits

Every photo is published with its photographer's credit:

- **On the image, the photographer's name exactly as they wrote it
  in the form — no handle, and not shortened.** If they wrote "Riddhee
  M", the image says "Riddhee M", not "Riddhee".
- **In the caption, the name AND the handle together** — e.g. "📷:
  Riddhee M @_ridzezey". If there is a name but no handle, the caption
  carries the name only.
- If neither a name nor a handle was given, the credit is "📷:
  Anonymous" in both places.
- If a submitter asked not to be named, don't name them.

The templates carry the credit as a prop — for example the campus
memory post shows "Photo: Harika Jakka" under the caption, and the
photo-of-the-day story shows "Photo: Anonymous" when no name was
given. A memory post presents the photo as a tilted (−3°) white
polaroid with a round-font caption; a story photo is full-bleed with
the caption in a white rounded card. See
[Posts](/docs/social-media-kit/posts) and
[Stories](/docs/social-media-kit/stories).

## Rules

- Don't stretch or squash photos.
- Don't put text over the busy part of a photo.
- Don't use a photo without knowing who took it, unless it is
  anonymous by the submitter's choice.
